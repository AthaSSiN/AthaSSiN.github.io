I am a PhD student at Princeton University, advised by Prof. Pramod Viswanath and Prof. Prateek Mittal. I study the reliability and security of language agents.

My work looks at how real-world agents are designed and deployed to find the ways they fail, such as prompt injection and context manipulation. I build automated red-teaming frameworks in which agents probe and evaluate other agents and their defenses, and I design defenses that work through a model's internal mechanisms to steer it toward desirable behavior.

Before Princeton, I was a quantitative researcher at A.P.T. Research in New Delhi, and I received my B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering from IIT Kanpur. In summer 2026, I interned at a stealth startup, working on the security of agentic systems.
